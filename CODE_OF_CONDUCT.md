# Code Of Conduct

This project aims to be a calm, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and specific
- Assume good intent
- Focus critique on code, design, and behavior
- Welcome questions from people who are still learning

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language
- Repeated hostile or dismissive behavior
- Deliberately disruptive conduct in issues or review threads

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or moderate comments, issues, or contributions that make collaboration unsafe or unproductive.
